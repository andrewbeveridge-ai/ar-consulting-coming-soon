The problem

A customer reaches out. Then what?

Most service businesses don't lose work because the work isn't good. They lose it in the gap between someone reaching out and someone following through. It usually looks like one of these.

The inquiry lands in an inbox

A form submission arrives between forty other emails. It gets read, mentally filed as "deal with that later," and later never quite arrives.

The response is slower than intended

You meant to call back that afternoon. The day ran long. By the time you get to it, they've already talked to someone else.

The callback gets forgotten

It was a note on a clipboard, a text to yourself, or something you were sure you'd remember. It's not that anyone was careless — there was just no system holding it.

The estimate goes out and goes quiet

You sent the number. They didn't reply. Nobody checked back in, so it sits there as neither a win nor a loss.

Inquiries arrive in different places

Website form, phone, a message on Facebook, a text to your cell. There's no single list, so there's no way to be sure the list is complete.

You can't see what happened to everything

You can remember the big jobs. Being able to look at every opportunity from the last thirty days and say exactly where each one stands is a different thing entirely.

None of this is a people problem. It's a missing-system problem — and that's a fixable one.

Never Miss a Lead is our first product: a straightforward workflow that runs behind your business, from the moment someone reaches out to the moment the opportunity is closed out one way or the other.
